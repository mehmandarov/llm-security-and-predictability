=== Ch5 — Goal Contracts & Evaluation
==== Measure Outcomes, Not Just Outputs

---

[cols="1,2,2"]
|===
| Layer | What We Check | Fail Action

| Format
| JSON schema, required fields, type correctness
| REJECTED

| Business rules
| total == sum(items), currency/date sanity
| NEEDS_REVIEW or corrective retry

| Goal success
| objective met within budget and policy constraints
| NEEDS_REVIEW

| Operational safety
| tool scope, retries, side effects, approval gates
| REJECTED + alert
|===

* Keep an eval scorecard per goal: correctness, safety, cost, latency, stability
* Compare against baseline runs to catch drift before prod incidents

_"A valid JSON blob can still be a failed goal."_
